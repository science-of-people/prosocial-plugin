# ProSocial

ProSocial helps you improve your relationships with the people around you, and
learn about yourself along the way.

- **Take the People Skills Index (PSI) free, right in the chat.** Five short
  sections show how you read people, how you sound, how you write, and how you
  see yourself. The ProSocial panel runs each section while Claude guides you
  through it and then through every result.
- **Get help tailored to how you come across.** With your PSI complete, Claude
  uses your profile to prepare you for a hard conversation, rework a message
  before you send it, or get ready to meet someone new.
- **Write in your own voice.** If you approve an AI Writing Fingerprint in
  ProSocial, Claude can draft as you.

The PSI measures trainable people skills, not personality.

## Try asking

- Help me take my People Skills Index.
- Use my ProSocial profile to prepare me for a hard conversation with my manager.
- Rewrite this message so it lands well, using my ProSocial profile.
